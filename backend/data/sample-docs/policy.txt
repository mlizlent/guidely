# Guidely — Company Policy

This document outlines Guidely's company policies. All employees are expected
to read and follow these rules. Managers may clarify any policy with HR.

## Code of conduct
Treat colleagues with respect, act with integrity, and keep confidential
information private. Harassment, discrimination, and retaliation are not
tolerated and may result in disciplinary action up to and including
termination.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week with manager approval.
During core collaboration hours, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, remote employees must be
reachable by chat, email, and video call. Employees are responsible for a quiet
workspace and reliable internet while working remotely.

## Vacation and sick leave
Employees accrue vacation days based on tenure and may request time off at
least two weeks in advance through the HR portal. Sick leave may be taken
short notice when illness prevents work. Extended medical leave requires
documentation and coordination with HR.

## Expense reimbursement
Submit expenses through the finance portal within 30 days of incurring the
cost. Include receipts for amounts over $25. Pre-approval is required for
equipment purchases over $500. Reimbursement is processed bi-weekly.

## Information security
Do not share credentials, use personal file-sharing services for company
data, or send sensitive information over unapproved channels. Report suspected
security incidents to IT immediately.

## Conflict of interest
Employees must disclose any outside business, investment, or relationship
that could influence their decisions at Guidely. Disclosures are reviewed by
the compliance team and kept confidential.

## Reporting violations
Concerns about policy violations may be reported to a manager, HR, or through
the anonymous ethics hotline. All reports are investigated promptly and
confidentially.
