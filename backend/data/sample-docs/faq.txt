# Guidely — Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the Guidely knowledge base. This document answers common questions
about working at Guidely. For questions not covered here, ask your manager or
open a ticket in the internal helpdesk.

## Working hours
Standard working hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM, Monday through Friday. Core
collaboration hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM, during which everyone is expected
to be reachable for meetings and Slack. Flexible start and end times are
available with manager approval.

## Remote work
Employees may work remotely up to three days per week. Remote days must be
scheduled in advance and communicated to the team. Employees on remote days
should be reachable by chat, email, and video call during core hours.

## Equipment
The engineering team provides a laptop, external monitor, keyboard, and
mouse. Personal equipment purchases require pre-approval from finance. Software
requests are handled through the IT request form.

## IT support
For login issues, printer problems, or software installation help, contact
IT support at it-support@guidely.com or open a ticket in the helpdesk. Most
requests are resolved within one business day.

## Time off
Vacation, sick leave, and holidays are governed by the company policy
document. Submit time-off requests at least two weeks in advance through the
HR portal.
